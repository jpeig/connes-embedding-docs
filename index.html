The entire operator-algebraic reduction — from a synchronous-game separation down to a tracial state that admits no trace-preserving embedding into a matrix ultrapower — is verified by the Lean 4 proof kernel against Mathlib. The one input from outside operator algebra, the MIP*=RE game separation, is isolated as a single named statement, and the kernel certifies that it is the only assumption beyond the logic itself.

1. What is here

Since Ji–Natarajan–Vidick–Wright–Yuen (MIP*=RE, 2020) the Connes Embedding Problem is known to have a negative answer, but the decisive input comes from quantum complexity theory — a field away from the operator-algebra community that owns the problem. To use ¬CEP one must take a long interactive-proof-theoretic construction on trust, and the operator-algebraic interface of the argument (the Kirchberg/Tsirelson passage, game algebras, amenable traces) has not been set down at a level of detail where each step can be inspected.

Where to start. Section 2 is one theorem and its four-line certificate; ten minutes. Section 8 is how to re-verify it yourself. For operator systems and spectral truncations, section 7 stands alone and is fully proved.

Three things are set out here, and one is deliberately not claimed:

  1. The main result (section 2). The reduction from the game separation to a non-embeddable tracial state is verified in full. The complexity-theoretic input enters at exactly one named statement — an axiom recording what MIP*=RE provides — and the kernel reports that nothing else beyond Lean's three standard axioms is assumed. The dilation and lifting arguments, finite-dimensional liftability, free-product permanence, the u.c.p. extraction and the final composition are all proved.

  2. A complexity-free architecture, with its open cores named (section 4). Two reduction chains toward ¬CEP that do not pass through MIP*=RE, through flexible Hilbert–Schmidt stability and the Dogon–Vigdorovich obstruction, each open ingredient carried as an explicit hypothesis rather than assumed silently.

  3. Why the purely operator-algebraic routes fail (section 6). Each known attack on an explicit non-hyperlinear group is dead, foreclosed, aimed at the wrong invariant, or exactly as hard as ¬CEP itself.

Section 7 is independent of all of this: a finite stage of the spectral-truncation program, at zero added axioms.

2. The main theorem

The statement, in FaithfulSingleCapstone.lean:

theorem cep_false_operator_algebraic_single_scaffold :
    ZFib.CEP.ConnesNonEmbeddableWitness

ConnesNonEmbeddableWitness asserts the existence of a C*-algebra carrying a tracial state that admits no trace-preserving embedding into a tracial matrix ultrapower — a witness refuting CEP. The predicate is not an abbreviation for something weaker: unfolded, it ends in matrix algebras, an ultrafilter, and an injective trace-preserving *-homomorphism, with the ultrapower pinned by an isomorphism to the concrete quotient of matrix sequences.

The single assumed input is stated where the theorem uses it:

/-- **Structured MIP*=RE citation.** The KPS/JNVWY synchronous separation, repackaged to
return the canonical universal measurement algebra with its `MeasurementAlgebraStructure`
(faithful: the qc-model construction IS on `C*(F(n,m)) ≅ ∗ⁿ Cᵐ`) together with the absence
of a u.c.p.-amenable (qa) trace. -/
axiom kirchberg_game_separation_ucp_structured : ...

In words: there is a finite synchronous game whose quantum-commuting model lives on the universal measurement algebra — the n-fold free product of m-outcome PVM algebras, C*(F(n,m)) ≅ ∗ⁿ Cᵐ — whose canonical trace admits no u.c.p.-amenable approximation. This is the content MIP*=RE supplies through the synchronous-games literature. It is stated here, not proved, so that the boundary between verified operator algebra and cited complexity theory is one statement a reader can find by name.

Everything downstream of it is verified, including free-product permanence of approximate u.c.p. liftability (Boca-dilation territory), proved internally as bocaFreeProductPermanence_holds_internal.

The certificate:

'ZFib.CEP.cep_false_operator_algebraic_single_scaffold' depends on axioms:
  [propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound,
   ZFib.CEP.kirchberg_game_separation_ucp_structured]

propext, Classical.choice and Quot.sound are Lean's three standard foundational axioms, present in essentially every development built on Mathlib. The fourth line is the game separation. There is no fifth line, and no sorryAx — see section 3 for what that means.

3. Reading a machine-checked claim, in one minute

Three notions suffice to audit what follows.

  • The kernel. A Lean proof is not accepted because a program judged it plausible: every proof is re-checked by a small trusted core. If the development compiles, each theorem in it is a theorem of the ambient logic, given the axioms listed for it.
  • An axiom is a citation. It states something without proof — the formal counterpart of "by [JNVWY 2020]". Such statements are tracked: #print axioms T reports every axiom the proof of T rests on, however deeply buried. A citation used three layers down still appears in the list.
  • A sorry is a gap, and it is visible. It marks an unfinished proof, and it is tracked the same way: anything depending on one reports sorryAx among its axioms. The list in section 2 contains none. The conditional chains of section 4 do contain sorryAx, by design — their gaps are the named statements listed in section 5.

So the audit is mechanical: check the development, print the axiom lists, read them. The audit script also fixes the main theorem's list, so any change that quietly enlarged it would fail to compile.

4. The complexity-free architecture

Can ¬CEP be reached without MIP*=RE, by exhibiting a non-hyperlinear group? That is open. What is formalized here is the sharpest reduction skeleton known to the author, with the open cores carried as explicit hypotheses — not as axioms, and not as gaps:

Chain A, rank one (ConditionalChain.lean):

theorem qce_implies_cep_false (p : Nat) [Fact (Nat.Prime p)]
    (hQCE : QCE_p p) : ConnesNonEmbeddableWitness

QCE_p is a dimension-uniform Hilbert–Schmidt rigidity statement on the congruence edge of the Bass–Serre amalgam SL₂(ℤ) ∗_{Γ₀(p)} SL₂(ℤ). From it: flexible HS-stability of the amalgam, then a Dogon–Vigdorovich central-extension obstruction forcing a non-hyperlinear group, then the group–von-Neumann dictionary yields the witness.

Chain B, higher rank (HighRankChain.lean):

theorem acm_implies_cep_false (L : HighRankLatticeData) (hACM : ACM L) :
    ConnesNonEmbeddableWitness

ACM (approximate congruence-monomiality) is the regular-sector half of flexible HS-stability for a higher-rank lattice; the remainder of the chain uses the Dogon–Vigdorovich 2025 higher-rank (T;FD) mechanism and the same shared tail.

Both compositions are free of gaps: what remains open are the named statements of section 5 and the two cores. Their status, established in section 6, is the reason these chains are presented as a map rather than a programme: the core of Chain A appears to be false — an exact multiplicity computation shows the torus-phase obstruction QCE_p would need has asymptotically vanishing relative mass — and the core of Chain B is ¬CEP-hard, since proving it would already produce the non-hyperlinear group. Formalizing the chains is what turns those diagnoses into sharp, checkable statements about where the difficulty sits.

The fully proved special case is the tempered subcategory in TemperedGap.lean — the 2×2 Gram positivity gap and the Deligne temperedness bound 2√p/(p+1) < 1 — with no gaps and no added axioms. It is a short starting point for a reader who wants to read one complete proof first.

6. Why the operator-algebraic routes fail

What makes ¬CEP resistant to a proof internal to operator algebras. Each route below is closed; the details are in the accompanying note.

  1. The core of Chain A fails structurally. The data distinguishing edge rigidity sits on a block of dimension Θ(k) inside congruence representations of dimension p^{k-1}(p+1) — vanishing relative mass. Optimal alignment succeeds asymptotically: the torus-phase obstruction washes out at rate √k·p^{-(k-1)/2}, confirmed numerically. So the rigidity QCE_p demands is not present. The reason is structural: SL₂(ℤ[1/p]) has no property (T), and nothing rigidifies the amenable congruence tower.

  2. Rigid, same-dimension stability is not available as an axis. By the Becker–Lubotzky mechanism an infinite hyperlinear property-(T) group cannot be HS-stable. The flexible notion is therefore the only live one — and it is exactly the one whose higher-rank instances are open.

  3. The core of Chain B is ¬CEP-hard rather than a reduction. Flexible HS-stability of a 2-Kazhdan higher-rank lattice — Dogon–Vigdorovich's own Problem 1.3 — would already produce an explicit non-hyperlinear group. The sharpest attack on it, a coupled op-norm/HS Hölder bootstrap against the (T;FD) spectral gap, does not close: the two halves require mutually exclusive regimes. The op-2 defect is rank-localized, and hiding it demands a growing complement, which destroys the Hölder constant the other half needs.

  4. The monomiality route rests on a false assumption. Approximate congruence-monomiality fails: cuspidal representations are not flexibly HS-monomializable within the required o(dim) budget.

  5. The non-soficity route aims at the wrong invariant. The cosystolic-expansion programme targets non-sofic groups; since sofic implies hyperlinear, a non-sofic group yields no non-hyperlinear group and no ¬CEP.

  6. The intrinsic invariants cannot see it. Cost, ℓ²-Betti numbers, cocycle superrigidity and sofic entropy are orbit-equivalence, ℓ²-spectral or sofic-relative quantities, whereas hyperlinearity is a trace-approximability property. Treeable relations are sofic at every cost and every ℓ²-Betti value, so no implication of the form "invariant ≠ expected value ⟹ non-hyperlinear" exists. The known candidate witnesses are built to satisfy Lück's determinant conjecture.

The value gap MIP*=RE detects is invisible to these invariants. That explains why a complexity-free proof has stayed out of reach, and why isolating the complexity input as one named statement, rather than obscuring it, is the useful interface for operator algebraists.

7. Spectral truncations as operator systems

Independent of CEP: a machine-checked finite stage of the spectral-truncation programme of Connes and van Suijlekom. Compress the shift algebra A = C*(S) on ℓ²(ℕ) by the spectral projection Q_M = 1_{[0,M]}(N) of the number operator. Verified, with no added axioms — Lean's three standard axioms only:

  • OS_M = Q_M A Q_M is a genuine operator system: unital, self-adjoint, and not closed under multiplication. The failure is proved, not asserted, at the cutoff mode: (Q_M S* Q_M)(Q_M S Q_M) ≠ Q_M (S*S) Q_M = Q_M, i.e. diag(1,…,1,0) ≠ diag(1,…,1) (T_FIB_OS_TRUNCATION_OPERATOR_SYSTEM.lean).
  • The Lipschitz seminorm L_M(a) = ‖[N_M, a]‖ lives on the shift, not the diagonal: [N_M, S_M] = S_M ≠ 0, while [N_M, ·] annihilates the diagonal masa. The full-band computation [N_M, S^p] = p·S^p is proved by induction on the band index, together with the separating-mode independence giving ker L_M = ℂ·Q_M for explicit finite band combinations (T_FIB_OS_FULL_BAND_KERNEL.lean).
  • The finite-stage Rieffel axioms hold for (OS_M, L_M): the *-seminorm axioms, kernel equals the scalars on the first band, and lower semicontinuity as closedness of the Lipschitz unit ball (T_FIB_OS_CQMS.lean).

One step here is stated and not proved: fibOSToep_normEquiv_constant, the finite-dimensional equivalence of norms giving ‖a'‖_op ≤ C_M · L_M(a') on the off-diagonal complement. It is an openly named hypothesis with its goal written out, it is not a gap in the sense of section 3, and it carries none of the results above; what follows from it is assembled conditionally on it.

Gromov–Hausdorff convergence of the truncated state spaces is not claimed. Transplanting the S¹ Fejér argument of Connes–van Suijlekom is the natural next step, and the one where a second pair of eyes would matter most.

Part II rests on a small self-contained ℓ²(ℕ) substrate (TruncationSubstrate.lean).

8. Checking it yourself

Everything needed is elan, the Lean version manager; the rest is fetched automatically.

lake exe cache get                      # Mathlib, prepared, once
lake build                              # check every proof
lake env lean scripts/AxiomCheck.lean   # print and freeze the axiom certificates

If the second command succeeds, every theorem is verified from the axioms listed for it. The third prints the certificates and fails if the main theorem's list ever differs from the four lines of section 2 — so that list is an invariant, not a claim in a README. The same three commands run automatically on every change, and the resulting axiom report is kept.

Verified against Lean v4.30.0-rc2 and Mathlib 1a5bbc7d3785.

9. Notes

The fib and ZFib name prefixes are a namespace convention and carry no mathematical content. Only the theorem and def statements are load-bearing, and the axiom certificates are the arbiter of what is proved.
