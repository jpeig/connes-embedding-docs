    def ZFib.Theorems.FibOSCQMSFull.FibOSFullRieffelCQMSFiniteStage (M : ℕ) :
    Prop

    The finite stage of the spectral-truncation programme, for the unilateral shift.

    Let S be the unilateral shift on ℓ²(ℕ) with orthonormal basis (eₖ), let N be the number operator, and for a cutoff M ≥ 1 put

    • Q_M = 1_[0,M](N) — the spectral projection onto the first M+1 modes,
    • S_M = Q_M S Q_M — the compressed shift, S_M* its adjoint,
    • N_M = Q_M N Q_M — the truncated number operator,
    • L_M(a) = ‖[N_M, a]‖ — the Lipschitz seminorm.

    This proposition is the conjunction of the four finite-stage Rieffel conditions for the pair (Q_M C*(S) Q_M, L_M), each written out rather than abbreviated:

    R0 — L_M is a ⋆-seminorm. 0 ≤ L_M a; L_M(c • a) = ‖c‖ · L_M a; L_M(a + b) ≤ L_M a + L_M b; and L_M(a*) = L_M a.

    R1 — the kernel is exactly the cutoff scalars. For the simultaneous all-band symbol a = c₀ Q_M + Σ_{1 ≤ p ≤ M} (c_p S_M^p + d_p (S_M*)^p), the commutator [N_M, a] vanishes on every basis vector precisely when c_p = d_p = 0 for every p. Equivalently ker L_M = ℂ · Q_M: the metric structure sees the off-diagonal bands and nothing else.

    R2 — weak-⋆ metrizability, in Rieffel's total-boundedness form. For every ε > 0 there is a finite set of centres such that every element of the Lipschitz unit ball lies within ε of one of them, modulo the cutoff scalars ℂ · Q_M.

    R3 — lower semicontinuity. The Lipschitz unit ball {a | L_M a ≤ 1} is closed.

    Not claimed here: Gromov–Hausdorff convergence of the truncated state spaces (S(Q_M C*(S) Q_M), d_{L_M}) to the limit. That is the natural continuation, in the manner of the Fejér-kernel argument for S¹.

    One point of care: Q_M is finite-rank and the Toeplitz algebra contains the compacts, so the corner Q_M C*(S) Q_M is itself an algebra. What fails to be multiplicative is the compression map — S_M* S_M = diag(1,…,1,0) ≠ Q_M, whereas Q_M (S*S) Q_M = Q_M.

      theorem ZFib.Theorems.FibOSCQMSFull.fibOS_fullRieffelCQMSFiniteStage (M : ℕ) :
      FibOSFullRieffelCQMSFiniteStage M

      The finite-stage Rieffel structure holds, for every cutoff, unconditionally.

      No domain-specific premise is used: the axiom footprint is [propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound], Lean's three standard axioms, with no sorryAx.

      Where each part comes from: R0 and R3 from the derivation algebra of [N_M, ·] and continuity of L_M; R1 from the band-eigen identities [N_M, S_M^p] = p · S_M^p and [N_M, (S_M*)^p] = −p · (S_M*)^p together with disjointness of the band supports; R2 from a finite-dimensional equivalence of norms on the off-diagonal complement, whose engine is injectivity of L_M there.
