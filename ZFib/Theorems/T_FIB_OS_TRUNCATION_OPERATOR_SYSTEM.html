            theorem ZFib.Theorems.FibOSTruncation.fibOS_truncation_non_multiplicative {M : ℕ} (hM : 1 ≤ M) :
            (fibCompSstar M).comp (fibCompS M) ≠ fibCutoffProj M

            NON-MULTIPLICATIVITY WITNESS (THE OPERATOR-SYSTEM SIGNATURE).

            (Q_M S* Q_M)(Q_M S Q_M) ≠ Q_M (S*S) Q_M = Q_M. Equivalently diag(1,…,1,0) ≠ diag(1,…,1), differing in the (M,M) entry.

            Proof: evaluate both sides at the top cutoff mode e_M. The compressed product gives 0 (fibCompSstar_comp_compS_basis at j = M, which is not < M), while Q_M e_M = e_M ≠ 0 (fibCutoffProj_basis, M ≤ M). Since S*S = 1 in A, the compression of the true product is Q_M 1 Q_M = Q_M, so the product of compressions differs from the compression of the product. OS_M is therefore not closed under multiplication.

            M ≥ 1 ensures e_M is a genuine cutoff mode (the witness is vacuous at M = 0 where the block is 1-dimensional).

            def ZFib.Theorems.FibOSTruncation.FibonacciOSTruncationFramework :
            Prop

            The Fibonacci OS-truncation operator-system framework. Bundles the operator-system structure of OS_M = Q_M A Q_M on the existing fibModeProj/fibNumberOpTrunc substrate:

            • (a) unital — the cutoff projection Q_M is the self-adjoint idempotent unit (Q_M 1 Q_M = Q_M);
            • (b) self-adjoint — S_M and S*_M are mutual adjoints (matrix-element form), so OS_M = OS_M*;
            • (c) NOT closed under multiplication — the van Suijlekom witness (Q_M S* Q_M)(Q_M S Q_M) ≠ Q_M;
            • (d) Lip-norm non-degeneracy on the shift — [N_M, ·] vanishes on the diagonal masa but acts as identity on the shift symbol S_M ≠ 0, so L_M(S_M) > 0 while L_M kills the diagonal (the metric algebra is the shift, not the diagonal).
